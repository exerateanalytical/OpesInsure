# OpesInsure — platform-critical control engines (owner, 2026-09-24)

Owner decision: **pause growth of the screen register (690) and formalize the insurance control engines first**; screens are derived from the engines afterwards.

## The seven core engines (sit under everything)
1. **Temporal insurance engine / reference-date engine** (item 45) — every decision resolves product rules, tariffs, treaties, provider contracts, taxes, templates and cover against the correct effective_at, not today's configuration.
2. **Coverage-at-loss engine** (9) with the **contract registry & legal chronology** (8) and **limit/aggregate exhaustion engine** (10) — reproduce exactly what cover existed at loss_datetime (effective dates, suspension, cancellation, endorsements, insured object, territory, limits, exclusions, waiting periods, deductibles, exhausted limits); never rely on policy.status == ACTIVE.
3. **Authority / delegation engine** (5, 6, 7) — intermediary authorization enforcement (current authorization, insurer mandate, product authorization, branch, jurisdiction, dates) and delegated authority limits (may_quote, may_bind, may_issue, max_sum_insured, max_discount, max_claim_approval, may_refund, may_override) with authority consumption and escalation (referral reason, recommendation, approval trail, return to originator).
4. **Regulatory-change engine** (2) — Regulatory Rule Registry DRAFT → REVIEWED → APPROVED → EFFECTIVE → SUPERSEDED with automatic impact on products, documents, tariffs, workflows, reports (CIMA regulations 2024, 2025, 2026).
5. **AML/CFT & compliance engine** (1, 18) — customer and beneficial-owner screening, PEP/sanctions/watchlists, source of funds/wealth, risk rating, EDD, periodic rescreening, alerts, suspicious-transaction cases, evidence retention, escalation; configurable rules (CIMA Regulation No. 003-25 on AML/CFT procedures); UBO/ownership graph.
6. **Case, task, diary & SLA engine** (24, 25, 26, 43) — shared case framework (owner, participants, status, tasks, evidence, correspondence, decisions, deadlines, audit) for underwriting referrals, suspicious claims, complaints, recoveries, litigation, provider disputes, compliance investigations; auto-generated tasks; business calendar (weekends, holidays, branch hours); operational queues per actor.
7. **Accumulation & catastrophe engine** (11, 12, 44) — exposure by zone/building/industrial area/flood zone/port/employer/event, gross and net of reinsurance; treaty-capacity check during underwriting (Within Retention / Covered by Treaty / Capacity Exceeded / Facultative Required); catastrophe event management.
