# Claims handler and adjuster guide

Claims officers, claims managers and loss adjusters work in the insurer portal (**/insurer**). Claims officers can also use the claims desk of the account area (**/account/claims-desk**), which works the same on the website and in the mobile app. You only see your company's claims.

## Getting started {#getting-started}

- Sign in at **/insurer/login** (or **/login** for the account area). **EN / FR** switches language.
- **Claims performance** and the **Claims dashboard** summarise the queue. Every step is an action in the claim's **Claim actions** menu; it appears only when your role allows it and the claim is at the right stage.

## Working the claims queue {#claims-queue}

1. Open **Claims** and filter the list (status, product, dates). **Register claim (FNOL)** records a new claim reported to you by phone or at the counter.
2. Open a claim. The tabs show Investigations, Experts, Parties, Coverage, Evidence review, Settlements, Carrier exchange and Litigation.
3. **Assign handler** gives the claim to a colleague (or yourself).
4. Check cover with **Run coverage check** and resolve it. Review the evidence (**Attach**, **Verify**, **Review evidence**) and ask for missing documents.
5. **Appoint expert** when an assessment is needed, then **Review expert report**.
6. **Record assessment** and **Review assessment**. **Request reserve change** sets the reserve; above your authority it waits for **Approve reserve change**.
7. **Propose decision**; a manager uses **Approve decision**.
8. **Calculate settlement**, **Offer settlement**, **Request discharge** and **Confirm discharge signed**, then **Request claim payment** (finance approves and pays).
9. Afterwards: **Record dispute** / **Resolve dispute**, **Request reopening** / **Decide reopening**, **Close / withdraw claim**. Recoveries use **Open recovery** and **Update recovery**.

## Approvals {#approvals}

- Reserves, decisions and payments above a person's authority go to the **Approval inbox**. Managers open the request and **Approve** or **Reject**; nobody can approve their own request.

## Adjuster workbench {#adjuster}

For loss adjusters and experts:

1. **Claims workbench** and **My assignments** list your missions.
2. **Accept assignment** (or **Decline assignment**), then **Schedule inspection**.
3. **Record field inspection** and **Add evidence** (photos, estimates).
4. **Build and submit report**, then **Submit recommendation**. **Completed assignments** keeps your finished missions.

## Health claims {#health}

- **Pre-authorization queue**: request information, propose a decision; an approver uses **Approve and issue guarantee of payment** or approves a stay extension.
- **Provider claims**: **Start review**, **Adjudicate**, then **Approve for payment**.
- **Provider disputes**: **Respond to dispute**.

## Claims desk (account area) {#claims-desk}

- **/account/claims-desk** ("Claims Management") shows your queue with tabs **All Claims**, **New Claims**, **Under Assessment**, **Pending Approval**, **Approved**, **Settled**, **Rejected**; search by claim number, policy or customer; **Export CSV**.
- Open a claim to **Assess Claim** (damage, repair estimate, recommendation), **Review Decision** / **Approve Claim**, **Reject Claim** or **Request More Information**, and **Process Payment**. **Payment Confirmed** shows the payment record and receipt.
