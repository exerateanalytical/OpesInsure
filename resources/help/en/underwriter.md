# Underwriter guide

Underwriters work in the insurer portal (**/insurer**). You price the risks that need a human decision, decide proposals and referrals, and authorise policy issuance for your company.

## Getting started {#getting-started}

- Sign in at **/insurer/login**. The **Underwriting dashboard** shows your workload; **Referrals** lists the cases referred by the rules.
- **EN / FR** switches language. Actions are in the menus of each record and only appear when your role and the record's status allow them.

## Quote requests {#quotes}

- **Quote requests** lists the risks brokers sent you for pricing. **Start work** to take one, or **Decline to quote**.
- Your quote is recorded on the broker's quote; **Quotes** shows your company's quotes read-only.

## Underwriting cases {#cases}

1. Open **Underwriting Cases**. Tabs: Unassigned, Overdue, Mine, Awaiting, All.
2. **Assign underwriter** (if you allocate work), then **Start review**.
3. **Run rules evaluation** to apply the eligibility and rating rules.
4. If you need more from the broker or customer, **Request information**; the case waits for the answer.
5. **Ready for decision**, then **Record decision** (accept, decline, or accept with conditions) or make a **Counter-offer**. **Resolve referral** closes a referral raised by the rules.

## Issuance {#issuance}

- **Policy Issuance Requests**: open a request and use **Issuance actions**: **Verify request**, **Request correction**, **Authorize & issue**. Some issues need a **Second approval** by another person; **Reject issuance** refuses it.
- **Issuance exceptions**: **Scan for paid-not-issued** finds paid proposals without a policy. On an exception, **Retry issuance**, **Escalate** or **Resolve**.
- **Policies** shows the issued policies. **Cancellation and suspension review**: **Review cancellation**, **Decide cancellation**, **Decide reinstatement**.

## Approvals {#approvals}

- Decisions above your authority are sent to the **Approval inbox** for a senior colleague. If you are the approver, open the request and **Approve** or **Reject**. You cannot approve your own request.

## Reinsurance and co-insurance {#reinsurance}

If your role includes it:

- **Treaty workbench**: treaties and versions, cession preview, **Cede policy**.
- **Facultative placements**: create a slip, record written lines, sign and submit, approve or reject.
- Reinsurers, recoveries and co-insurance arrangements are in the same menu group.
