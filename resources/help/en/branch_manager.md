# Branch manager guide

As branch manager you use the broker portal (**/broker**) for your own branch only: you see the staff, agents, customers and cases of your branch and nothing from other branches.

## Getting started {#getting-started}

- Sign in at **/broker/login**. Your membership must be linked to a branch; if **My branch** is missing, ask your broker administrator to set the branch on your membership.
- **EN / FR** switches language; **Global search** finds records of your branch.

## My branch {#overview}

- **My branch** (**/broker/branch**) is your overview. The tiles show your branch staff, open cases, cash to review and open sticker counts.
- The table lists the staff and agents of your branch.
- The page is read only: use it to see where work is piling up and follow up with the people concerned.

## Your branch's book {#branch-book}

- **Quotes**, **Proposals**, **Policies**, **Renewal Cases** and **Claims** show the records of your branch, with the actions your role allows. The steps are the same as in the **Broker staff** guide.
- **Claims dashboard** summarises the branch's claims.

## Leads {#leads}

- **Leads** lists your branch's prospects.
- **Lead assignment**: **Assign lead** gives a prospect to one of your producers so it is followed up.

## Stickers and cash {#stickers}

- The **My branch** tiles show open sticker counts and cash to review. Stickers are handed over and reconciled from **Sticker allocation** and **Sticker reconciliation** when your role allows it.
