# Institutional Data Provenance

## Insurers

The seed contains 18 non-life and 11 life entries from the Association of Insurance Companies of Cameroon directory checked on 22 September 2026.

- Non-life: <https://www.asac-cameroun.org/groupes/assureurs-non-vie/>
- Life: <https://www.asac-cameroun.org/groupes/assureurs-vie/>

The life and non-life companies are separate institutional entries even when they share a group brand.

## Brokers

The broker seed is derived from MINFI’s publication titled “Liste des sociétés de courtages autorisées à présenter les opérations d’assurances au public — année 2022,” including its addendum.

Source: <https://minfi.gov.cm/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/liste_des_societes_de_courtages_autorises_a_presenter_les_operations_d_assurances_au_public_annee_2022.pdf>

This is historical official data. It is not safe to label a record “currently licensed” until MINFI supplies or confirms a current register. Production import must retain the original row number, name, address, manager, phones, source publication, effective period and review evidence.

## Verified public offers

Only product information supported by an official company page is included in the sample:

- Chanas: motor, travel, home and health.
- SanlamAllianz Cameroon: motor, travel, home, HospiCare, education, family protection, savings and retirement.
- AXA Cameroon: Elite Voyage/medical concierge information.

No price is seeded. Product availability can change and must be revalidated through the carrier or connected ERP before marketplace publication.

## Logos

The scaffold deliberately uses neutral letter marks. A logo may be added only when it comes from the carrier/broker, an approved brand kit or an official source whose reuse is authorised. Store its provenance, checksum, approval, version and expiry. Never hotlink an unverified logo or infer a brand mark from search results.
