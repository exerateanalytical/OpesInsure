# Broker administrator guide

As broker administrator you run your brokerage in the broker portal (**/broker**): you see the whole company's book, manage your staff, and oversee KYC, finance and settlements with insurers. Everything a producer does is also open to you – see the **Broker staff** guide (tab above) for the day-to-day sales and servicing steps.

## Getting started {#getting-started}

- Sign in at **/broker/login**. The **Dashboard**, **Customer dashboard** and **Claims dashboard** cover the whole company.
- **Organisation settings** holds your company's details; as administrator you can edit them.
- **Reports** gives production and finance reports; the **Partner workspace → Reports** link opens the book reports in your account area.

## Staff and access {#staff}

- **Staff** (**/broker/memberships**) lists everyone in your brokerage with their role and status (filter Active / Suspended / Revoked).
- **Invite a staff member** on that list sends an invitation with a role. The person accepts it and signs in with their own account.
- You can also invite from the account area (**/account/staff**). Roles decide what each person sees: producers see their own records, supervisors their team, branch managers their branch.

## Sales oversight {#sales}

- **Quotes**, **Proposals**, **Policies**, **Renewal Cases** and **Claims** show the whole company's book, with the same actions as for staff.
- **Premium overrides**: **Decide premium override** approves or refuses a producer's request, then **Apply approved premium override**.
- **Leads** and **Lead assignment**: **Assign lead** gives a prospect to a producer.
- **Payment dashboard**: follow payments, **Retry payment**, **Request refund**.

## Customers and KYC {#kyc}

- **KYC dashboard**, **KYC queue** and **KYC case** follow the identity checks of your clients.
- **KYC remediation** and **Expiring documents**: **Start remediation**, **Attach document**, declare source of funds or wealth.
- **Corporate due diligence** records the ownership of corporate clients.
- **Duplicate customers**: **Scan for duplicates**, dismiss a candidate or **Request merge**.
- **Portfolio transfer**: **Transfer portfolio** moves clients from one producer to another.

## Finance with insurers {#finance}

- **Bordereaux**: **Prepare bordereau**, **Approve bordereau**, **Submit to insurer**.
- **Settlements**: prepare, calculate and submit a settlement for review; after approval submit it for payment and confirm it paid.
- **Commission receivables**, **Partner Statements** and **Partner Payout Requests** show commission earned, statements and payout requests (**Request payout** on a published statement).
- **Contracts & delegated authority** shows your agreements with insurers (read only).

See the **Finance** guide for the detailed finance steps.
