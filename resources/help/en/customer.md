# Customer guide

This guide covers your OpesInsure account on the website (**/account**). The same account works in the mobile app.

## Signing in and your dashboard {#getting-started}

- Sign in at **/login** with your mobile number. If you have no account yet, use **/signup**.
- After sign-in you land on the **Dashboard** (**/account**). It summarises your policies, claims and payments.
- Use the left menu to move between **My Policies**, **Quotes**, **Claims**, **Payments**, **Documents**, **My Vehicles**, **Policy requests**, **My Profile**, **Identity check**, **Privacy & security**, **Notifications** and **Support**.
- Switch language at any time with **EN / FR** in the top bar.
- Sensitive actions may ask you to **Confirm with a code**: enter the 6-digit code sent to you by SMS.

## Buying insurance {#buy}

Start from **Quotes → New quote** or **/account/buy** ("Create a New Quote"). The journey has six steps:

1. **Risk details** – tell us what to insure (for example your vehicle).
2. **Coverage** – choose your cover.
3. **Compare quotes** – review and compare the offers of the insurers.
4. **Customize plan** – adjust options and confirm.
5. **Review & payment** – check the summary and pay.
6. **Confirmation** – the policy is issued.

After confirmation your policy is active from the start date shown, its documents appear under **Documents**, and the certificate can be verified by the authorities with its number.

## Paying {#payments}

- Go to **Payments** to see your payments, or open a policy and pay what is due (**Make a Payment**).
- **1. Select Payment Method**: **MTN Mobile Money** or **Orange Money** (other methods appear only when your insurer offers them).
- Enter the payer number and approve the prompt on your phone. A receipt is issued once the payment completes.
- If a payment failed, retry it: a new payment prompt is sent to your phone.

## Your policies and documents {#policies}

- **My Policies** lists each policy with its number, insured item, start and end dates, premium and certificate.
- Open a policy to see its details, download documents and make requests (for example a change or a cancellation). Follow them under **Policy requests**.
- **Documents** keeps every document issued to you (policy schedules, certificates, receipts). Download them at any time.
- **My Vehicles** lists the vehicles linked to your motor policies.

## Filing a claim {#claims}

1. Go to **Claims** and choose **Start a New Claim** ("File a New Claim").
2. **Policy Selection** – choose the policy concerned.
3. **Incident Details** – describe what happened, when and where.
4. **Documents** – upload photos and supporting documents.
5. **Review & Submit** – check and submit.

You can save a draft and **Resume** it later. Each claim shows its progress: **Claim Submitted → Documents Received → Under Assessment → Approval → Payment**. If documents are requested, upload them from the claim page. While a claim is still open you can use **Withdraw Claim**.

## Profile, identity check and privacy {#profile}

- **My Profile** – your contact details and preferences.
- **Identity check** – submit your identity document when asked (KYC). Some purchases need a verified identity.
- **Privacy & security** – your consents and account security. To delete your account, use **/account/delete**.
- **Notifications** – messages about your policies, payments and claims (the bell in the top bar shows unread ones).

## Getting help {#support}

- **Support** lets you contact the OpesInsure team; you can also use **/contact**.
- This help page is always available at **/account/help**. Use the search box to find a topic and the **Printable / PDF version** link to print the guide.
