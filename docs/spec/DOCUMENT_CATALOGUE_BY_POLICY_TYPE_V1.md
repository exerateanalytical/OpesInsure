# OpesInsure Insurance Document Catalogue by Policy Type — owner specification v1.0 (2026-09-24)

Full 60-section text supplied by the owner in session 2026-09-24. Locked essentials:

- **Six groups:** pre-contract · contract/policy · certificates/proof of cover · servicing · claim · financial/settlement. Documents may be mandatory, conditional, internal, broker, claim-generated or class-specific.
- **Universal pre-contract:** QUOTE (Devis), INSURANCE_PROPOSAL (Proposition — not the policy), RISK_DECLARATION, PRODUCT_INFORMATION, UNDERWRITING_REQUIREMENTS, CONDITIONAL_OFFER.
- **Universal policy:** POLICY (CIMA Art. 8 content), POLICY_SCHEDULE (Conditions particulières), GENERAL_CONDITIONS, SPECIAL_CONDITIONS, COVER_NOTE (Note de couverture), PREMIUM_RECEIPT (Quittance de prime).
- **Per-class packs** (owner lists 4–44): motor new business (incl. attestation retained by owner and detachable certificate displayed on vehicle — CIMA Arts. 213, 214, 221; provisional attestation), motor renewal, motor endorsement (avenant), motor claim (15 insurer documents); individual health, corporate/group health (master + member certificates/cards), health treatment/cashless (eligibility, preauthorization, guarantee of payment, admission/extension, EOB …); life new business (incl. LIFE_CONTRACT_SUMMARY per Art. 65-1), beneficiary, surrender, policy loan/advance, maturity, death claim; property/home and property claims; business multirisk; travel (incl. visa/Schengen certificate, assistance card); personal accident; group PA; group life (member document required); credit life; funeral; retirement/savings/capitalization (statements, withdrawals, advance, maturity); marine cargo (open cover, shipment certificates) and cargo claims; marine hull; construction/CAR; machinery/engineering; professional liability; public/general liability; intermediary PROFESSIONAL_LIABILITY_CERTIFICATE (first-class, annually renewed); surety/bonds; credit insurance; agriculture; livestock; aviation; cyber; school; hotel/hospitality; fleet (master policy + per-vehicle attestation and certificate).
- **Universal servicing (14), financial (10) and claim (22) document types** as listed in sections 45–47.
- **Origin vs issuer:** document_origin INSURER, BROKER, CUSTOMER, PROVIDER, GARAGE, ADJUSTER, AUTHORITY, BANK, REINSURER, SYSTEM; document_stage PRE_CONTRACT, UNDERWRITING, POLICY, SERVICING, RENEWAL, CLAIM, SETTLEMENT, FINANCE, COMPLIANCE. Customer/third-party evidence stored separately from insurer-generated documents.
- **Engine rule:** Insurance class → product → lifecycle stage → document requirement (REQUIRED / CONDITIONAL / PRODUCT_DEPENDENT / where applicable) → template → template version → generated document. Never "one policy PDF".
- **DOCUMENT_PACK** (e.g. MOTOR_NEW_BUSINESS_PACK, LIFE_NEW_BUSINESS_PACK): one "Download Policy Pack" action, each document kept separately.
- **Template ownership** PLATFORM, INSURER, BROKER, REGULATORY; carrier contractual templates override platform templates; insurer controls legal wording; FR, EN or bilingual.
- **Generated document record:** template_id, template_version, product_version, policy_version, generated_at, issuer, language, hash, verification_code; later template changes never alter issued documents.
- **Statuses:** VALID, SUPERSEDED, REPLACED, REVOKED, EXPIRED, CANCELLED — never delete issued documents.
- **CIMA registry:** continuous numbering; policy → endorsement 001 → 002 → renewal kept as one traceable contract history.
- **Catalogue size:** ~180–250 canonical document types (pre-contract 15–25, contract 25–40, certificates 15–25, servicing 20–30, renewals 10–15, claims 35–50, finance 20–30, life servicing 15–25, health/provider 20–30, corporate/group 15–25, reinsurance/co-insurance 20–30, compliance 20–30); an individual policy typically issues 5–12 at inception.
- **Per product, answer:** before insurance · when cover begins · proof of cover · contract changes · renewal · claim · money paid · policy ends.
