# Insurer administrator guide

The insurer portal (**/insurer**) is your company's workspace on OpesInsure. As insurer administrator you manage your staff, your products and tariffs, your agreements with brokers and your company settings, and you follow the portfolio. You only ever see your own company's data.

## Getting started {#getting-started}

- Sign in at **/insurer/login** (password reset on the same page). **EN / FR** switches language; your profile is in the user menu.
- The **Dashboard** and the dashboards **Production**, **Portfolio**, **Claims performance**, **Broker production** and **Product performance** show your figures.
- **Global search** finds a policy, claim, quote, customer or document of your company.
- Changes are made through the named actions of each record (for example **Catalogue actions**). If an action is missing, your role does not grant it or the record is not in the right status.

## Staff and organisation {#users}

- **Staff invitations**: **Create** an invitation with the person's contact and role; **Revoke** an invitation that should not be used. You cannot grant a role above your own.
- **Staff** lists your people and their status.
- **Organisation settings**: your company's details (editable with the organisation management permission).
- **Letterhead & logo** sets the letterhead printed on your documents; a new version may need approval.

## Products and tariffs {#products}

1. **Insurance Products**: open a product. The tabs show Overview, Governance, Completeness, Changes, Tests, Timeline and Documents.
2. Use **Catalogue actions** to build it: **Add plan**, **Set plan coverages**, **Configure coverage**, **Add limit**, **Add deductible**, **Attach exclusion**. Run the product tests, then advance it for review and **publish** it (or **reject** it).
3. **Tariff Versions**: **Submit for review**, **Approve tariff**, **Schedule tariff**, **Activate tariff**; **Expire tariff** ends a version.
4. **Eligibility rules** and **Rating rules** hold the rules applied when brokers and customers quote; **Simulate** tests a rule set before approval.

Only published products with an active tariff can be sold.

## Distribution {#distribution}

- **Contracts & delegated authority**: **New agreement** with a broker, **Set product permission** for the products it may sell, **Change agreement status**.
- **Intermediaries** lists the brokers and agents working with you.
- **Bordereaux** received from brokers: **Record acknowledgement** or **Record rejection**.

## Portfolio oversight {#portfolio}

- **Policies**, **Claims** and **Quotes** show your company's portfolio. Quotes are read-only here; underwriters handle quote requests.
- **Policy Issuance Requests** and **Issuance exceptions** show what is waiting to be issued (see the **Underwriter** guide).
- **Sticker Batches** and **Sticker Stocks** follow your motor stickers (**Hand over stickers**, **Reconcile stickers**).
- **Document register**: **Upload** an insurer original, **Download**, **Verify** or revoke / replace a document.

## Approvals {#approvals}

- The **Approval inbox** collects decisions that need a second person (above someone's authority). Open a request, check the details, then **Approve** or **Reject**. The requester can **Withdraw** it.
- You cannot approve your own request.
