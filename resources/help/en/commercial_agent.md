# Commercial agent guide

As a commercial agent you work from your account area (**/account**), on the website or in the mobile app. You only ever see the clients attributed to you. The agent menu entries appear once you are signed in with an agent role.

## Your workspace {#getting-started}

- Sign in at **/login**. Your role is shown under your name in the top bar ("Agent / Broker").
- The agent menu adds **Clients**, **My Book**, **Action Centre**, **Tasks & follow-ups**, **Leads**, **Reports**, **Commissions** and **Stickers** to the customer entries.
- Some sensitive actions ask you to **Confirm with a code** sent by SMS.

## Leads: from prospect to client {#leads}

1. Open **Leads** (your sales pipeline).
2. Use the **New lead** form and **Add lead** to capture a prospect.
3. Move the lead forward as you follow it up.
4. When the prospect agrees, choose **Convert to client**. Confirm that the prospect has given consent to be registered. The prospect then appears in **Clients**.

## Clients {#clients}

- **Clients** lists your clients with their policies, KYC status and next renewal. The **Renewal soon** tab shows policies ending within 60 days.
- Open a client to see profile, policies and quotes, and the sub-pages activities, KYC, vehicles, payments and claim.
- **New client** starts a quote for a person not yet in your book.

## Quotes and sales {#quotes}

- From a client choose **New quote for client**, or use **New quote**. The quote journey is the same as for customers: **Risk details → Coverage → Compare quotes → Customize plan → Review & payment → Confirmation**.
- Open a quote to send it to the client or mark it as lost. Proposals collect the client's information and documents before issue.
- Payment is made by mobile money (MTN Mobile Money or Orange Money); the client approves the prompt on their phone.

## My Book, renewals and servicing {#book}

- **My Book** shows the quotes, proposals, policies and claims of the clients attributed to you.
- From a policy you can open its documents and start an endorsement or a cancellation request.
- Watch **Upcoming renewals** (policies ending within 60 days) and contact the clients concerned.
- **Action Centre** and **Tasks & follow-ups** list what needs your attention.
- **Stickers** shows the motor stickers available to you.

## Claims for your clients {#claims}

- From a client or from **My Book**, you can declare a loss on behalf of a client. The claim is recorded as agent-assisted.
- Add evidence (photos, documents) from the claim page. The insurer's claims team assesses and decides the claim.

## Reports and commissions {#commissions}

- **Reports** shows your production figures computed from your book (for example quotes by status).
- **Commissions** shows the commission earned on your policies and your **Withdrawals** to mobile money.
